One integration point, every sensor

A single unified embedded framework absorbs thermal, vibration, current, and voltage. New modality = driver module, not a new stack.

One data lake, one customer portal

Every sensor lands in one clean data-lake schema under Connect. Every customer monitors and controls their fleet through one portal, backed by the Hub at the edge.

Proactive AI, edge to cloud

Local models on the Hub catch failures before they happen, not after. Fleet models in Connect turn that into prescriptive maintenance: less downtime for customers, a lighter long-term footprint for the whole stack.
